# Format: long-form

More than 3 minutes, usually 16:9, watched deliberately. Layer on any playbook; it adds structure around the beats.

## Hook

- The first minute is where viewers leave. Pay the title's promise in that minute, with a strong image or the answer's first half.
- Do not open with channel intro, sponsor or a recap of the topic.
- State what the viewer will have at the end in one sentence.

## Beat length

- Beats run 20 to 45 seconds; one idea each.
- Re-hook every 60 to 90 seconds: a new question, a reversal, or a promise of what comes next.
- Add a stronger re-hook around the three-minute mark, where a second wave of viewers drops.
- Group beats into 3 to 5 chapters with short titles.

## Ending

- Resolve the opening question explicitly, then one sentence of what changes for the viewer.
- Suggest a single next video or action, not a menu.
- Let the last image hold for 3 seconds.

## On-screen text

- Chapter titles on the chapter's first frame; lower thirds for sources and names.
- Key numbers get a large frame; everything else is picture and voice.

## Captions

- Provide a caption track (sidecar subtitle file) so viewers can turn it on or off.
- Break lines at phrase boundaries, up to 42 characters per line.
