# Format: no-voice

Music and on-screen text only, no narration. Layer on any playbook; text becomes the voice.

## Hook

- The first frame has the hook as text, 6 words or fewer, or a striking image with a one-line caption.
- Music starts at frame zero; silence is also a choice, but then the first text must appear in under 1 second.

## Beat length

- Cut on the music: beat changes land on downbeats or phrase starts.
- Each text frame is readable in its time at a normal reading speed: about 3 words per second, minimum 1.5 seconds.
- Fewer, longer beats than a voiced version; drop beats that need explanation.

## Ending

- Last text frame holds for 3 seconds, then the music resolves.
- The call to action is text only, one line.

## On-screen text

- Max 6 to 8 words per frame, one idea each; kinetic type carries stress by size and timing.
- Sources and numbers appear as small labels near the picture they belong to.
- Text takes the role the narrator's "but" and "therefore" had: write them out ("Nhưng...", "Vì vậy...").

## Captions

- Text is the content, so there is no separate caption track; check that it survives crops for 9:16 and 16:9.
- Describe sounds that matter in picture notes (`> sfx:`) for the soundtrack, not on screen.
