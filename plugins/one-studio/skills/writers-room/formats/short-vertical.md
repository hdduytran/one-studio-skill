# Format: short-vertical

9:16, under 60 seconds, watched on a phone, often with sound off. Layer on any playbook; it compresses the beat table, it does not replace it.

## Hook

- The hook lands in 1 to 3 seconds: a question, a stake or a surprising fact. Never a greeting or the product name.
- The first frame already carries text, because many viewers have sound off. Voice and on-screen text say the same hook in different words.
- Pay the hook's promise within the video; do not save it for a follow-up.

## Beat length

- 5 to 7 beats, each one or two sentences, 4 to 8 seconds.
- Every beat earns the next second: it ends on a "but" or "therefore" that pulls the eye forward.
- Merge the playbook's smallest beats; keep the percentages, drop the weakest beat entirely.

## Ending

- One call to action, spoken once, shown once.
- Where it fits, finish on a line or image that connects back to the first frame so a replay feels natural. This is a common technique with no measured effect; do not promise results.

## On-screen text

- Max 6 to 8 words per frame, large, in the safe area away from platform buttons (bottom 20% and right edge).
- Text replaces the voice only for the hook and the key number; do not mirror every sentence.

## Captions

- Burn captions in; keep them to two lines, one idea per line.
- Highlight the stressed word, not every word.
- Proofread the Vietnamese diacritics on the final render.
