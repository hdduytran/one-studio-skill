# Playbook: review-teardown

## Use when

Judging a product ("is it worth buying?") or dissecting one to see how it is built: a phone, a tool, a subscription, a gadget. The viewer is about to spend money or time.

## Viewer's question

"Should I buy it, who is it for, and what is the catch?"

## Story core questions

- What is the verdict in two or three sentences, before any detail?
- Who is it for, and who should walk away?
- What was the testing: how long, in what setting, against what comparison?
- How did we obtain the product (bought, loaned, sponsored)? State it.
- What does the teardown show that the marketing does not?

## Beat skeleton

Verdict first, then details, balanced pros and cons, then a clear final recommendation. Stating the drawbacks plainly raises trust. Disclose how the product was obtained, and compare with products of the same tier for context.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdict | 10 | Two or three sentences and who it is for | opens |
| How I tested | 8 | Duration, setting, disclosure | THEREFORE this is what I saw |
| Best thing | 15 | The strongest advantage, shown | BUT it has a price |
| Inside (teardown) | 20 | Open it; what the build tells about the claims | THEREFORE the cost is explained |
| Worst thing | 17 | The real drawback, shown | BUT does it matter to you |
| Versus peers | 12 | Same-tier alternatives, fair | THEREFORE who should choose which |
| Final recommendation | 12 | Buy / wait / skip, by situation | closes |
| Silence | 6 | Scorecard stays on screen | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, nồi chiên không dầu):
1. Kết luận: đáng mua nếu bạn nấu cho một đến hai người, bỏ qua nếu nhà có năm người.
2. Mình dùng nó bốn tuần, máy tự mua, không ai tài trợ.
3. Nhưng bên trong, nắp lọc dầu chỉ là một tấm nhựa mỏng.
4. Vì vậy sau ba tuần nó ám mùi, và đó là điểm yếu nhất.
5. So với hai nồi cùng tầm giá, nồi này gọn hơn nhưng chiên ít hơn.

## Hook types

- Verdict: "Đáng mua, nhưng chỉ nếu bạn thuộc đúng một nhóm người."
- Inside secret: "Mở nắp ra mới thấy vì sao nó rẻ hơn đối thủ ba trăm nghìn."
- Disclosure: "Mình tự mua, dùng bốn tuần, và có một lỗi mình không tha thứ được."

## Visual modes

- exploded-cutaway: parts pulled apart and labelled in the order they matter to the verdict.
- split-compare: this product against peers on one task.
- data-story-motion: scorecard rows appearing one at a time.
- screen-cinema: when the product is software, a real recorded run.

## Research needed

- Your own use log: dates, tasks, numbers (battery hours, noise, time to boil).
- Spec sheet and price on a given date; peer prices the same day.
- Teardown or component sources (service manuals, public teardown sites) for any inside claim.
- Disclosure facts: purchased, loaned, affiliate links.

## Traps

- Burying the verdict after five minutes of unboxing.
- All praise and one soft "minor" con; the worst real flaw needs its own beat.
- Judging from spec sheet numbers you did not measure.
- A scorecard dumped at once; reveal each row as you speak about it.
- Comparing a mid-range product against a flagship without saying so.
- Hiding an affiliate link or free unit in the description only.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The verdict is in the first 20 seconds.
- [ ] The worst flaw has its own beat and a shown example.
- [ ] How the product was obtained is stated.
- [ ] Peers are the same tier and the same date.
- [ ] Recommendation branches by buyer type.
- [ ] Every measured number is yours or sourced.
