# Playbook: video-essay

## Use when

An argued opinion with a thesis someone could disagree with: "why we are wrong about X", a critique, a cultural take. The voice is the product. If the user has no real position, do not write it.

## Viewer's question

"Why should I believe this, and what is the best argument against it?"

## Story core questions

- What does the author actually believe? Ask the user; never invent a view.
- What is the thesis in one disputable sentence?
- What is the strongest counter-argument, in its holder's best words?
- What evidence is first-hand (an experience, a document, a measurement)?
- Where does the nuance land at the end: what is conceded?

## Beat skeleton

Name a contestable thesis early, then support it in but/therefore order, then face the strongest opposing case, then a conclusion with nuance. The essay is a chain of reasons, not a pile of examples.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thesis | 8 | The disputable claim in one sentence | opens |
| Why people believe the opposite | 12 | Give their view fairly | BUT it has a crack |
| Evidence 1 | 18 | First-hand or documented, sourced | THEREFORE the crack widens |
| Evidence 2 | 18 | A different kind of proof | BUT this could be a coincidence |
| Strongest counter-argument | 18 | Best words of the other side | THEREFORE what survives? |
| Reply | 12 | Answer it, concede what must be conceded | THEREFORE the claim is narrower |
| Conclusion with nuance | 10 | The refined thesis and what to do with it | closes |
| Silence | 4 | One held image | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, "làm việc nhóm không tạo ra ý tưởng tốt hơn"):
1. Tôi nghĩ các buổi họp brainstorm làm ý tưởng của bạn tệ đi.
2. Ai cũng tin nhiều đầu tốt hơn một đầu, và tôi cũng từng tin.
3. Nhưng ở công ty tôi, ba mươi ý tưởng đầu tiên đều đến từ một người nói to nhất.
4. Vì vậy tôi thử ghi ý tưởng riêng trước, rồi mới họp.
5. Phản biện mạnh nhất: người nhút nhát sẽ bị bỏ lại. Tôi đồng ý một nửa.

## Hook types

- Disputable claim: "Họp brainstorm làm ý tưởng của bạn tệ đi."
- Self-correction: "Tôi đã tin điều ngược lại suốt mười năm."
- Question the crowd: "Tại sao mọi người đều đồng ý với một điều chưa ai kiểm tra?"

## Visual modes

- desk-collage: clippings, quoted passages with sources, highlighter on the key phrase.
- kinetic-type: the thesis and the pivotal sentence with stress and pause.
- archive-documents: the study, the email, the page being argued about.
- split-compare: the claim against the counter-claim.

## Research needed

- The author's actual opinion, confirmed with the user in their words.
- The opposing view from its strongest advocate, quoted with a link.
- Primary evidence for each step: studies, documents, firsthand logs.
- Anything that would change the conclusion, listed honestly.

## Traps

- A thesis nobody could disagree with ("communication is important").
- Setting up a straw man as the opposing side; use its best quote.
- Presenting a single anecdote as proof of a general claim without saying so.
- Generating conviction the author does not have; the voice reads as hollow.
- Ending with a call to action that contradicts the nuanced conclusion.
- Quote chains with no source, copied from other essays.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The user confirmed the position in their own words.
- [ ] The thesis can be argued against.
- [ ] The opposing view is quoted from its strongest source.
- [ ] Each evidence beat links by "but" or "therefore".
- [ ] A concession appears in the conclusion.
- [ ] Opinions are labelled as such; facts carry sources.
