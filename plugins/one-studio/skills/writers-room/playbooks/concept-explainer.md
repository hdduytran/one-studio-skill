# Playbook: concept-explainer

## Use when

Explaining an abstract idea or term to beginners: inflation, entropy, an API, compound interest, a design pattern. There is no machine to follow (that is mechanism-system); the work is changing how the viewer thinks.

## Viewer's question

"What does this word actually mean, and why was my guess wrong?"

## Story core questions

- What misconception do most people hold? Find it in real posts, not guesses.
- What is the smallest concrete example that already contains the idea?
- What visual metaphor carries it, and where does the metaphor break?
- What human-scale anchor makes the size or scale felt?
- What is the one sentence the viewer should be able to say to a friend?

## Beat skeleton

State the misconception, let the viewer sit unsure for a moment, then break it. Example first, rule next, name last. Tell it as a path of discovery ("suppose you were the first to meet this problem"), second person.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Misconception stated | 12 | Say the common belief as if true | opens |
| Test that it fails | 15 | A concrete case where the belief gives the wrong answer | BUT it does not hold |
| Smallest example | 20 | Numbers small enough to do in the head | THEREFORE a pattern appears |
| The rule | 18 | Name the pattern in plain words | BUT does it scale |
| Scale anchor | 12 | Human-size comparison | THEREFORE the surprise |
| The technical name | 8 | Now give the term, once | THEREFORE you can search for it |
| One-sentence takeaway | 10 | The line to repeat to a friend | closes |
| Silence | 5 | Metaphor image holds | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, lãi kép):
1. Nhiều người nghĩ gửi mười triệu, lãi sáu phần trăm thì sau mười năm được thêm sáu triệu.
2. Nhưng năm thứ hai, lãi đã tính cả trên phần lãi của năm đầu.
3. Vì vậy sau mười năm bạn có gần mười bảy triệu chứ không phải mười sáu.
4. Với cùng số tiền, nếu sống ba mươi năm, con số là năm mươi bảy triệu.
5. Cái tên của hiện tượng này là lãi kép.

## Hook types

- Wrong belief: "Bạn nghĩ gửi mười triệu, sáu phần trăm, mười năm thì được thêm sáu triệu? Gần đúng, và đó là vấn đề."
- Scale: "Một tờ giấy gập đôi bốn mươi hai lần sẽ chạm tới Mặt Trăng."
- Challenge: "Đoán nhanh trước khi tôi nói đáp án."

## Visual modes

- world-camera: infinite-zoom or a scale ladder anchored to human size.
- match-cut-motif: one object (a coin, a sheet) changes state at each step.
- kinetic-type: the misconception and the takeaway as short on-screen text.
- split-compare: belief against reality side by side.

## Research needed

- The misconception in real forum or survey text, with a link.
- Textbook or primary definition, and one worked example you recomputed yourself.
- Where the popular metaphor misleads; one expert source.
- Numbers with unit and date.

## Traps

- Opening with the dictionary definition; it gives nothing to be wrong about.
- A clear explanation with no wrong belief confronted; clear lectures leave the misconception intact.
- A metaphor that fails at the first follow-up question and is never acknowledged.
- Naming the technical term in the first 30 seconds and losing beginners.
- Examples with ugly numbers; use 10, 100, 1000 until the pattern is seen.
- Stacking three metaphors; pick one and keep it.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The misconception is stated before it is broken.
- [ ] A worked example the viewer can follow in their head.
- [ ] The term appears after the example.
- [ ] The metaphor's limit is admitted.
- [ ] The takeaway fits in one spoken sentence.
- [ ] Every number was recomputed.
