# Playbook: customer-story

## Use when

One real customer's before and after: case study, testimonial, success story. The viewer is a prospect who wants to see someone like themselves go through the problem.

## Viewer's question

"Did this work for somebody like me, and what did it cost them to try?"

## Story core questions

- Who is the customer (role, size, place) and what did they actually say in the interview?
- What was the specific problem, in their words, and what did they fear about switching?
- What was the turning point: the day or event that made them act?
- What is the one hard result (a number with a baseline and a period)?
- Does the customer allow name, face, number to be used? Written consent.

## Beat skeleton

The customer is the hero and the brand is the guide (the "Yoda, not Luke" rule). Enter the conflict within the first 20 seconds of a two-minute video. For a short cut use Before, After, Bridge.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer in their world | 12 | Who, where, one ordinary detail | opens |
| The problem | 18 | Concrete, in their words, with a cost | opens conflict |
| The fear | 12 | Why they hesitated | BUT they had tried the usual |
| Turning point | 15 | The day it changed | THEREFORE they tried it |
| What changed | 18 | Two or three real moments, same frame as before | THEREFORE the week looks different |
| Result | 15 | One hard number, baseline and period | BUT a number is not the point |
| Their advice | 7 | One sentence to someone in their position | closes |
| Silence | 3 | Let the face breathe | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, quán cà phê chuỗi nhỏ):
1. Anh Hùng có ba quán, và mỗi tối đóng cửa anh đếm tiền bằng tay đến mười giờ.
2. Cuối tháng lệch năm triệu mà không ai biết lệch ở đâu.
3. Nhưng anh sợ đổi phần mềm, vì lần trước nhân viên mất hai tuần mới quen.
4. Vì vậy anh thử ở đúng một quán, đúng một tuần.
5. Tháng sau số liệu lệch còn dưới trăm nghìn, và anh về nhà trước chín giờ.

## Hook types

- Their sentence: "Mười giờ đêm, anh Hùng vẫn đang đếm tiền bằng tay."
- Number with a baseline: "Lệch năm triệu mỗi tháng, và không ai biết ở đâu."
- The fear: "Lần trước đổi phần mềm, cả quán mất hai tuần để quen."

## Visual modes

- world-camera: the customer's real place as one continuous world; camera moves through their day.
- split-compare: same frame before and after, same hour.
- data-story-motion: the result number moving against its baseline.
- kinetic-type: their strongest sentence, verbatim.

## Research needed

- Recorded interview or transcript; their words, not paraphrase.
- Baseline, result and time period from the customer's own records.
- Written permission for name, logo, face and figures.
- What they used before and why it failed, as they describe it.

## Traps

- The brand speaking for the customer; if the voice says "our solution", the hero changed.
- Praise without a baseline ("great improvement"): a number with no "before" proves nothing.
- Starting with the company history, so conflict arrives after 40 seconds.
- Polished stock footage in place of the customer's real counter, desk, screen.
- A result that is really a vendor-wide average, not this customer's.
- Cutting the fear; the prospect shares it, so removing it removes the reason to trust.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Conflict appears within the first 20 seconds.
- [ ] At least two verbatim customer sentences.
- [ ] The hard number has baseline, period and source.
- [ ] The brand appears only as the guide, never as the hero.
- [ ] Consent is recorded before the script locks.
- [ ] The before frame and after frame match in composition.
