# Playbook: brand-story

## Use when

Who we are and why we exist: founder story, manifesto, about-us film, anniversary. The viewer is a customer, candidate or partner deciding whether they trust and like this company.

## Viewer's question

"Why does this company exist, and is it on my side?"

## Story core questions

- What was the moment of origin: a specific day, a failure, an overheard sentence?
- What does the company believe that its competitors would argue with?
- Who is the hero of the film: the customer, with the brand as guide?
- What is the proof of the belief (what the company did when it cost them)?
- What would the founder say off the record that is not in the corporate deck?

## Beat skeleton

Origin, belief, what we do, proof. A film that reads a list of values fails. The founder tells why, not achievements.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin moment | 20 | One day, one place, one problem | opens |
| What was wrong with the world | 15 | The thing people accepted | BUT someone should have fixed it |
| The belief | 15 | One sentence stated plainly | THEREFORE we started |
| What we do | 15 | Shown through a customer's hands, not a list | THEREFORE it looks like this |
| Proof | 20 | A time the belief cost something | BUT it was worth it because |
| Invitation | 10 | What we ask of the viewer | closes |
| Silence | 5 | Hold on a human face or place | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, xưởng bánh gia đình):
1. Năm 2012, mẹ tôi dậy lúc ba giờ sáng để nhào bột trong căn bếp hai mét vuông.
2. Ai cũng nói bánh mì ngon thì phải dùng chất làm mềm.
3. Nhưng mẹ không tin, vì bà nhớ bánh của bà ngoại để ba ngày vẫn mềm.
4. Vì vậy tụi tôi mất hai năm thử men ủ lạnh.
5. Có năm giá bột tăng gấp đôi, tụi tôi vẫn giữ công thức, và mất ba khách lớn.

## Hook types

- Origin scene: "Ba giờ sáng, căn bếp hai mét vuông, và một người không chịu dùng chất làm mềm."
- Contrarian belief: "Mọi người bảo bánh ngon thì phải có phụ gia. Chúng tôi không tin."
- Cost: "Năm đó tụi tôi mất ba khách lớn chỉ vì không đổi công thức."

## Visual modes

- world-camera: the place of origin as one continuous world the camera walks through over time.
- match-cut-motif: one object (the dough, the first sign) from past to present.
- archive-documents: first invoice, photo, hand-written recipe, with sources.
- kinetic-type: the one-sentence belief.

## Research needed

- Founder interview, recorded; use their words and hesitations.
- Dated documents and photos from the origin (with permission).
- Evidence for the proof moment: a decision, a figure, an affected person.
- What customers say the company stands for, from reviews.

## Traps

- Reading the values list ("chính trực, sáng tạo, tận tâm"); each word is true of every company.
- The founder bragging about awards and revenue instead of the day it nearly ended.
- Drone shots of a building in place of the human who started it.
- A belief stated without a cost; a belief that never cost anything is just a slogan.
- Over-polished TTS narration of a raw story; leave pauses and short sentences.
- Using adjectives where one concrete detail (the 2 m2 kitchen) would do.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The origin is a specific day, not "since our founding".
- [ ] The belief fits in one sentence a rival would dispute.
- [ ] The proof shows the belief costing something.
- [ ] No list of values spoken.
- [ ] Customer, not the brand, is the hero of the "what we do" beat.
- [ ] At least one pause or silence on a human face.
