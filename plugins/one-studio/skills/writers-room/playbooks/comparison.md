# Playbook: comparison

## Use when

A versus video: product A against B, build against buy, two approaches, old way against new way. The viewer is choosing and wants the criteria that decide it, not a feature grid.

## Viewer's question

"Which one should I pick for my situation, and what will I regret?"

## Story core questions

- Who is the viewer, and which situation flips the answer? (Team of two, or two hundred.)
- Which two or three criteria actually decide this choice?
- What is the other side's best honest argument?
- Is there a data source from a third party, or is it our own test?
- Can we name competitors? Without third-party data, say "the usual way" instead of naming a logo.

## Beat skeleton

Same task, same clock, criteria revealed one at a time. Present the other workflow fairly: a straw man ruins the whole video.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| The choice | 10 | The situation and what is at stake | opens |
| The task | 8 | One real task both must do, rules of the test | THEREFORE we run both |
| Criterion 1 | 18 | Run it, result on screen | BUT the second one flips |
| Criterion 2 | 18 | Different kind of cost (time, money, risk) | THEREFORE the picture changes |
| Criterion 3 | 14 | The one most people forget | BUT it matters only if... |
| The other side's best case | 10 | Fair point for the loser | THEREFORE it depends on you |
| Verdict by situation | 14 | "If you are X choose A; if Y choose B" | closes |
| Silence | 8 | Table stays on screen | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, tự dựng server hay thuê cloud):
1. Công ty năm người, một hạn chót, hai cách chạy ứng dụng.
2. Cùng một việc: triển khai bản cập nhật lúc hai giờ chiều.
3. Nhưng máy tự dựng chạy xong sau chín phút, còn cloud mất bốn mươi giây.
4. Vì vậy câu hỏi thật là ai trực lúc ba giờ sáng.
5. Nếu bạn dưới mười người, thuê cloud; trên đó thì tính lại.

## Hook types

- Stake: "Chọn nhầm cái này, đội bạn trả thêm hai tháng lương mỗi năm."
- Race: "Hai cách, một đồng hồ. Ai xong trước không phải là câu trả lời."
- Surprise verdict: "Cái rẻ hơn lại là lựa chọn tốt hơn, trừ khi bạn thuộc ba nhóm này."

## Visual modes

- split-compare: same task, two panels, one shared clock; criteria row appears only as it is tested.
- screen-cinema: real runs of both when software.
- data-story-motion: cost over time as the criteria reveal.
- kinetic-type: the verdict sentence.

## Research needed

- Official docs and pricing pages with access date for both options.
- Third-party benchmarks or reviews; your own test with method written down.
- Complaints and praise from real users of each, with links.
- Anything that changed recently (price, version) that invalidates old comparisons.

## Traps

- Dumping the full comparison table at once; reveal one row as the voice reaches it.
- Testing A on a tuned setup and B on defaults; state the setup or the result is worthless.
- Declaring a winner without saying for whom; the verdict must branch by situation.
- Naming a competitor and criticising it with only your own claims.
- Outdated pricing; cite the date shown on the pricing page.
- Ending on a feature count ("A has 12, B has 9") that decides nothing.

## Review checklist

- [ ] The test conditions are shown on screen or spoken.
- [ ] Each criterion is one beat with one result.
- [ ] The loser gets its best argument in its own terms.
- [ ] Verdict branches by viewer situation.
- [ ] Every price and number has a source and a date.
- [ ] No competitor logo without third-party data.
