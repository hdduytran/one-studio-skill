# Playbook: data-story

## Use when

A finding in numbers: a survey, a market report, a trend over years, an internal metric turned into a story. The viewer needs to feel one counterintuitive number and trust where it came from.

## Viewer's question

"What does this number mean, why is it not what I expected, and can I trust it?"

## Story core questions

- Which single number contradicts what the viewer assumes?
- What is the time span, population and unit, exactly?
- Where is the break point in the series, and what happened there?
- What is each chart's one-sentence conclusion, written as its title?
- Who collected the data, how, and what does it leave out?

## Beat skeleton

Video is an author-led genre (Segel and Heer): strict order, strong message. The martini-glass shape: a linear stem, then a wide mouth where the final frame holds long enough to read. Rosling's lesson: narration moves with the animation, effects mark the event, and play, pause and rewind of time are storytelling tools.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| The counterintuitive number | 10 | One number against the common belief | opens |
| How it was measured | 8 | Source, sample, unit, in one sentence | THEREFORE you can trust it, within limits |
| Data runs through time | 25 | Animate the series; narration synchronised | BUT then something breaks |
| Pause at the break | 15 | Freeze time, mark the event | THEREFORE we ask why |
| Explanation | 18 | One or two causes with evidence | BUT correlation is not everything |
| Caveat | 8 | What the data cannot say | THEREFORE the safe claim is... |
| Back to the full picture | 8 | Zoom out to the whole chart | closes |
| Held final frame | 8 | Stay long enough to read it | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, giá thuê nhà):
1. Giá thuê phòng trọ tăng ba mươi phần trăm, nhưng lương chỉ tăng chín.
2. Số liệu lấy từ hai nghìn tin đăng, từ 2019 đến 2024, ở một thành phố.
3. Nhưng năm 2022 đường cong gãy: giá nhảy một lần rồi đi ngang.
4. Vì vậy chúng ta dừng ở đúng tháng đó và hỏi chuyện gì xảy ra.
5. Dữ liệu này không nói được giá ở quận khác; kết luận dừng ở đây.

## Hook types

- Contradiction: "Giá thuê trọ tăng ba mươi phần trăm. Lương tăng chín. Khoảng cách đó là một tháng tiền ăn."
- Break point: "Đường cong này đi thẳng suốt ba năm, rồi gãy ở một tháng."
- Human anchor: "Nếu bạn đi làm từ 2019, đây là khoản bạn đã mất."

## Visual modes

- data-story-motion: series animated with narration; freeze at the break; highlight effect at events.
- split-compare: two series on one axis only when the contrast is the point.
- kinetic-type: the headline number and the chart title as a sentence.
- desk-collage: the source document or report page the figure came from.

## Research needed

- The dataset or report: publisher, date, sample size, method, definitions.
- Recompute at least the headline number from raw data or a second source.
- What changed at the break point (policy, event) with a dated source.
- Limits the publisher itself states.

## Traps

- A chart with a truncated axis that makes a 3% change look like a cliff; start at zero or say so.
- Titles like "Price over time" instead of the conclusion as a sentence.
- Dual axes that let any two lines look correlated.
- Showing all series at once; reveal them one at a time as the voice reaches them.
- Rounding in the voice differently from the chart, so the viewer hears 30% and reads 28.6%.
- Ending on the chart before the viewer can read it.

## Review checklist

- [ ] One headline number against one common belief.
- [ ] Source, population, unit and period are spoken once.
- [ ] Every chart has a sentence as its title.
- [ ] Time stops at the break point.
- [ ] Voice and chart use the same rounded figure.
- [ ] Final frame holds at least 3 seconds.
