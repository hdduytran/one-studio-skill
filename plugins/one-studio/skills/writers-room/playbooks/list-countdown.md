# Playbook: list-countdown

## Use when

A ranked or numbered list for social: top 5 mistakes, 7 tips, best tools, a countdown. The viewer will swipe away unless each item earns the next seconds.

## Viewer's question

"Is there something in this list I did not already know, and is the best one worth waiting for?"

## Story core questions

- What single question does every item answer?
- What is the ranking rule (surprise, cost, frequency), stated once?
- Which item is best, and how do we promise it at the start (open loop)?
- Does each item have a claim, a concrete image and a reason?
- Which items are filler and must be deleted?

## Beat skeleton

A list needs a spine: one shared question, items rising in surprise, the best saved for last and promised from the first second. Each item is one claim, one concrete visual and one reason.

| Beat | % of runtime | Job | but / therefore link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promise | 8 | The question plus the open loop for item 1 | opens |
| Item at the bottom | 14 | Claim, image, reason; least surprising | THEREFORE the next is stronger |
| Middle items (3 to 4) | 54 | Each rising in surprise, each different in kind | BUT this one is worse than it looks |
| Near-best | 10 | Almost the winner; sets up the reveal | THEREFORE the winner has to beat this |
| The best | 12 | Reveal with the strongest image | closes the loop |
| Silence | 2 | One frame | n/a |

Example outline (Vietnamese, năm lỗi khi học tiếng Anh một mình):
1. Năm lỗi làm bạn học mãi không tiến, và lỗi cuối cùng làm tôi mất hai năm.
2. Lỗi năm: học từ vựng theo danh sách, vì não quên sau ba ngày.
3. Nhưng lỗi bốn tệ hơn: chỉ đọc, không bao giờ nói thành tiếng.
4. Vì vậy lỗi ba còn đau hơn: xem phụ đề tiếng Việt.
5. Lỗi một: chờ đến khi đủ giỏi mới nói. Đây là lỗi tôi mắc hai năm.

## Hook types

- Open loop: "Năm lỗi học tiếng Anh. Lỗi cuối cùng làm tôi mất hai năm."
- Rule of the list: "Mình xếp theo mức độ tốn thời gian, từ ít đến nhiều."
- Challenge: "Nếu bạn mắc cả năm lỗi, bạn thuộc nhóm chín mươi phần trăm."

## Visual modes

- kinetic-type: the item number and its claim, held big for the first second.
- match-cut-motif: a countdown object that changes state with each item.
- desk-collage: one concrete image per item, cut paper style.
- split-compare: the mistake against the fix.

## Research needed

- Each item verified by a source or first-hand log; list the source per item.
- Ranking evidence: a count, a cost, a survey, or the author's labelled judgement.
- A concrete example image for every item.
- Check no two items are the same item in different words.

## Traps

- Padding to reach a round number; "five" with two weak items is worse than "three".
- Revealing the best item first or having no promise at the start.
- Items that are all the same kind (five tools), so nothing rises in surprise.
- Spending twenty seconds on the weakest item to hit a length.
- A countdown with no ranking rule, so the order feels arbitrary.
- Abstract item statements ("be consistent") with no image or example.

## Review checklist

- [ ] One question that every item answers.
- [ ] Ranking rule stated in one sentence.
- [ ] The best item is promised in the first 5 seconds.
- [ ] Each item has a claim, an image and a reason.
- [ ] No filler item; delete the weakest if unsure.
- [ ] The last item pays off the opening loop.
